- **New name: LRCStatus.** Same app: it updates over your current install and keeps your settings.
- **Tabs**: Now, Setup, Status, Lyrics and More, so there's much less scrolling.
- **Genius**: Search lyrics also shows songs from Genius. Genius lyrics aren't synced, so they can't move your status line by line, but you can read them and use them, and Poweramp shows them when LRCLIB has nothing. Turn it off under Lyrics → Lyrics Sources.
- **Updates**: the app looks for a new version when it opens (every launch, once a day, once a week or never, under More) and shows a notice with a Download button. "Check for updates now" does it straight away.
- **More → Links & Updates**: buttons for the GitHub page and smegolas.com, and **Add to Obtainium** for automatic updates.

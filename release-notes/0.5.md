- Two themes for the app: Classic, styled like Discord's settings, and CS 1.6, styled like the Counter-Strike 1.6 menus.
- The CS 1.6 theme can play menu sounds when you tap buttons. You add the sound files yourself.
- Optional filter that hides swears and slurs in your status (e.g. f***), plus your own list of words to hide.
- Lyrics delay slider: the minimum time between status updates, with 3 seconds marked as recommended to avoid Discord rate limits. Lines that come too fast are shown together instead of being skipped.

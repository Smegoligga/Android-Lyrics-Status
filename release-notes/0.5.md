## 🎨 Themes

There's a new theme picker. Each theme rebuilds the whole screen in its own style.

- **Classic (Discord)** is the default and is built like Discord's settings:
  - Discord's dark or light palette, following your phone.
  - Grey uppercase section labels over rounded panels.
  - Blurple buttons, green ✓/✕ toggles and dark text boxes.
- **CS 1.6** is the Counter-Strike 1.6 / Half-Life VGUI look from smegolas.com:
  - Same colours as the site, with an olive gradient background.
  - A main menu that jumps to each window, ending in **Quit**.
  - Every section is a bevelled window with a title bar. Its `x` box rolls the window up.
  - VGUI buttons that sink when pressed, checkboxes, radio buttons, and text boxes that go yellow when focused.

## 🔊 CS 1.6 menu sounds

These are the game's menu sounds, played the same way as on smegolas.com:
- a click when you press,
- a release sound when you let go,
- a rollover sound on hover.

They're off by default, with a volume slider. The `.wav` files are Valve's, so they aren't shipped with the app. Import them once under *Options → Audio → Import sound files…*, from smegolas.com or your Half-Life folder (`steamapps/common/Half-Life/valve/sound/UI`).

## 🤐 Censoring

- **Censor swears and slurs** masks them in your status, like `f***` or `bulls***`. Innocent words like *class* or *Scunthorpe* are left alone.
- **Custom word filter** masks your own comma-separated words, with its own toggle.
- Both are off by default. With them off, nothing is censored.

## ⏱️ Lyrics delay

- **Lyrics delay** (under Timing) replaces *Min. seconds between updates*. It's now a slider with a **recommended 3 s** marker, and you can tap *Recommended* to use it. It sets the minimum time between status changes, which keeps almost every line while avoiding the bursts that get you rate limited by Discord. Longer delays also save battery.
- **Merge skipped lines** (on by default): lines the delay skips are shown together, like `line A / line B`, instead of being dropped.
  - Only lines skipped by the delay are merged, not ones you seek past.
  - If the result is longer than Discord's limit, the newest lines are kept.

## 📥 Install

Download `Android-Lyrics-Status-0.5-release.apk` below and open it on your phone. It installs over v0.4 and keeps your settings.

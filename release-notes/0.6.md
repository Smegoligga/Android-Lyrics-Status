## 🔊 Menu sounds fixed

Imported CS 1.6 menu sounds didn't play. There were two causes:

- **The sounds used the system/touch volume.** Many phones keep it at zero or mute it in Do Not Disturb. They now play at your **media volume**, like on smegolas.com.
- **Importing didn't switch them on.** Menu sounds are off by default, so even after a successful import nothing played. Importing the files now turns menu sounds on automatically.

*Options → Audio* also shows the state of each file: **ready**, **loading…**, **not added**, or **can't be played** if Android can't decode it. A bad file is now easy to spot.

## 📥 Install

Download `Android-Lyrics-Status-0.6-release.apk` below and open it on your phone. It installs over v0.5 and keeps your settings and imported sounds.

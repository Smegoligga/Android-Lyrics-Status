## 🔀 New name, new focus: Android Lyrics Status

**LyricsStatus for Poweramp is now _Android Lyrics Status_.**

- **Works with any music app.** It follows whichever app is playing: Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music, Samsung Music, local players and more. It uses Android's media controls, then finds lyrics in your files or on LRCLIB.
- **Poweramp stays the fully supported player**, with extras the others don't get:
  - **Exact file path.** `.lrc` files and embedded lyrics are always found. Other players only get them when the song is in your music library.
  - **Poweramp lyrics plugin.** LRCLIB lyrics also appear inside Poweramp's own lyrics view.
  - **Deep sleep.** With *Only follow Poweramp* on, the app shuts down completely while Poweramp is idle and wakes when you press play.
- **"Only follow Poweramp" is now off by default** for new installs. If you used an earlier version, your saved choice is kept. Switch it in *General*.
- If several apps are playing, Poweramp wins. A paused Poweramp no longer blocks another app that's playing.
- **The update installs over v0.3** and keeps your settings and token. The app's internal ID is unchanged.

## 🔋 Battery

- **Sleeps while idle.** After 10 minutes without playback, in Poweramp-only mode, Android can stop the app entirely. Poweramp wakes it when you press play.
- **Fewer notification wakeups.** On Android 13+, the app no longer receives every notification, only ongoing ones like the media player.
- **No polling.** During playback the app wakes only when the next line is due. It used to wake every second.
- **One connection to Discord.** It's reused instead of opening a new encrypted connection for every line.
- **No storage write per line.**
- **New battery saver setting: _Min. seconds between updates_.** Set it to 5–10 s to skip fast lines and cut network use while music plays. The default of 0 sends every line, like before.
